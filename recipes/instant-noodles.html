Instant noodles Recipe

Description

Iconic instant noodles known for their quick preparation and signature masala flavor. This classic recipe provides a savory, warm bowl of noodles with a spiced broth.

Ingredients

  • 1 packet instant noodles (70g)
  • 1 sachet Maggi Tastemaker (included in the packet)
  • 210-250 ml Water (approx. 1 to 1.5 cups)
  • Optional: 1 tsp butter or oil for a richer texture

Steps

  1. Boil Water: Pour the water into a pan and bring it to a rolling boil over high heat.
  2. Add Ingredients: Break the noodle cake into four parts and add them to the boiling water along with the Tastemaker powder.
  3. Cook for 2 Minutes: Cook in an open pan for exactly 2 minutes, stirring occasionally to separate the strands.
  4. Finish & Serve: Turn off the heat. Do not drain the remaining water, as it forms the flavorful sauce. Let it sit for 1 minute to thicken before serving hot.
